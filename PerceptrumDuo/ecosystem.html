Perceptrum Ecosystem

Artistic, scientific and institutional relationships that contribute to the ongoing evolution of Perceptrum.

Perceptrum is conceived as an evolving artistic ecosystem. Just as the Sounding Canvases establish dialogues between paintings, visitors and distant exhibition spaces, the project itself develops through exchanges with artists, researchers, institutions and audiences.

🎨

Artistic Community

Perceptrum grows through ongoing conversations with fellow artists, curators, and independent exhibition spaces. We view our creative network not as a list of collaborators, but as a reciprocal environment that challenges our assumptions of the interface.

Scientific Research

We do not use science to validate art; rather, science becomes another lens through which we observe human perception. Current inquiries focus on psychoacoustics and machine learning models that interpret gesture history, with future developments oriented toward EEG integration and the objective mapping of visitor experience.

📚

Academic Practice

Perceptrum is a form of artistic research. Our findings are distilled into papers and talks that bridge the gap between aesthetic theory and technical implementation.

Institutions

The project actively seeks long-term institutional relationships with museums, galleries, and cultural centers. We are interested in spaces that view the exhibition as a laboratory for the post-immersive era.

🌍

Future Network

Distributed exhibitions connecting Barcelona, London, Tokyo, and New York through a real-time, interconnected sensory system.
