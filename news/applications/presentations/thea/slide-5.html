Fusion for Energy (F4E) FPGA Pipelines & Control Systems

FPGA based pipelines: Developed custom FPGA-based CompactRIO analog optical modules and backplane diagnostic tools, engineering a 5 MS/s data streaming pipeline with custom start-of-frame byte interleaving, optical-to-chassis routing, and LabVIEW signal analysis for low-latency acquisition.

Engineering & Commissioning: Performed on-site testing, commissioning, troubleshooting, FAT/SAT, and PLC/HMI debugging for ITER plant systems, buildings, and electrical distribution networks using Siemens TIA Portal and PLCSIM Advanced.

Control Systems Integration: Deployed and validated UCL, ICL, and ACL components, ensuring correct HMI displays, interlock propagation, and data exchange across plant subsystems.
